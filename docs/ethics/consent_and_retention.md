# ZECPath AI — Consent, Privacy and Data Retention

## 1. Candidate notice and consent
Before AI-assisted screening, candidates should receive a clear notice
explaining:
- The purpose of AI processing.
- The categories of data processed.
- How AI-generated scores and recommendations may be used.
- Whether any processing is optional and how consent is handled.
- How to ask questions or raise concerns.

The application must implement the applicable notice and consent
requirements. A policy file alone does not prove enforcement.

## 2. Explainability and human oversight
Recruiters should be able to review score components and job-related
evidence. AI-generated recommendations should support, not replace,
appropriate human decision-making.

## 3. Data retention
The current policy requires a documented retention period, a deletion
process, access controls, and audit logging.

The retention period is currently unset. The responsible privacy or
legal reviewer must establish an appropriate period based on the
purpose of processing, applicable law, and operational requirements.

## 4. Required implementation checks
- Identify where resumes, parsed profiles, transcripts, scores, and
  interview reports are stored.
- Document retention rules for each data category.
- Verify deletion and access-control behavior.
- Verify that audit logs do not unnecessarily expose candidate data.
- Test consent enforcement at the relevant processing entry points.
