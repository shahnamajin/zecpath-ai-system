# ATS Improvement Backlog

## 1. Improve Experience Matching

Some candidates may receive lower scores because their experience descriptions use different terminology from the job description.

### Improvement

Improve semantic experience matching and synonym handling.

---

## 2. Improve Non-Tech Role Support

Test additional non-technical roles such as:

- HR
- Marketing
- Sales
- Finance
- Project Management

### Improvement

Add role-specific skill and experience mappings.

---

## 3. Improve Fresher Evaluation

Freshers may have limited professional experience but strong academic and project backgrounds.

### Improvement

Increase the importance of education, projects, certifications, and skills when professional experience is missing.

---

## 4. Improve Senior Candidate Evaluation

Senior candidates should receive appropriate consideration for years of experience and leadership responsibilities.

### Improvement

Improve experience relevance scoring.

---

## 5. Reduce Semantic Mismatches

Investigate cases where semantic similarity does not correctly reflect job relevance.

### Improvement

Tune similarity thresholds and evaluate additional embedding models.

---

## 6. Expand Testing Dataset

Increase the number of resumes tested across different industries and experience levels.

### Improvement

Build a larger labeled validation dataset.

---

## 7. Improve Role Adaptability

Continue testing role-specific scoring weights.

### Improvement

Add and validate more role configurations.
